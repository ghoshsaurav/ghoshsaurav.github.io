  1. Poster
    Peak-a-Boo: Making Binary Chromatographic Peak Detections Reviewable
    Saurav Ghosh, Saugat Pandey, and Alvitta Ottley
    Nov 2026
    Poster presented at IEEE VIS 2026, Boston, MA
    Abs DOI Bib HTML Poster Website

    Peak-a-Boo is a visual analytics prototype that prioritizes ambiguous chromatographic peak detections for review. It shows evidence such as local contrast, noise, and detection stability, and lets analysts explore detector settings in an interactive sandbox. The prototype was tested on synthetic chromatograms.
